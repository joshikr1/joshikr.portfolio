About Me

I'm a Mechatronics Engineering student at UNSW Sydney. Since joining Bluesat, UNSW's student space society, I've taught myself lead screw mechanics and gear ratios from scratch to lead the Z-axis design of a micro resin 3D printer using Onshape and Fusion 360 for CAM. Previously, I also used torque calculations and Onshape to design a counterweight system for a mobile autonomous antenna system for tracking satellites in Low Earth Orbit, including helping out with waterproofing the electronics for outdoor operation.


I gravitate toward projects that force me to learn fast: I was a Track Winner at the Cambridge EduX Hackathon, and placed in the top 50 globally at Microsoft's AI for Good Hackathon, devising an AI system to speed up emergency response times. Right now I'm designing a prosthetic thumb for my grandfather, the most personal engineering project I've taken on.


I also mentor high school Maths and Physics students and train new tutors, which keeps me sharp at explaining technical ideas clearly, something I bring into every engineering team I'm part of.


I'm currently at UC Berkeley on exchange till December 2026, and am looking for robotics/mechatronics internship opportunities to build on this experience.

Projects

CubeSat Resin Printer — Z-Axis Mechanism

Mechanical design lead for the Z-axis mechanism, Bluesat (UNSW's student satellite society). Designed a dual-lead-screw Z-axis for a micro resin 3D printer payload on a 3U CubeSat/AURC rocket, targeting 50-micrometre accuracy under 30g launch loads. CAD, analysis, and manufacturing are complete and flight testing is underway.

📂 View gallery & docs

ATFL Ankle Exoskeleton

Designing a wearable exoskeleton boot for ATFL ankle ligament injuries. Two linear actuators actively correct ankle inversion, promoting blood flow to the injury site while mechanically bypassing load off the ligament, addressing a gap in current treatment, where rigid immobilization is shown to slow recovery.

Autonomous Fire-Detection Rover

Designing the turret (2 degrees of freedom) for an autonomous rover that detects and extinguishes residential house fires, using temperature sensors, infrared sensors, and a camera.
