About Me

I am a second-year Mechatronics Engineering student at UNSW Sydney, currently on exchange at UC Berkeley studying Robotic Locomotion under Professor Homayoon Kazerooni, Mechatronics Design under Professor Hannah Stuart and Fluid Mechanics under Professor Shawn Shadden.


Recently promoted to Mechanical Lead at Bluesat, UNSW's Student Space Society, after teaching myself lead screw mechanics and gear ratios from scratch to design the Z-axis mechanism of a micro resin 3D printer CubeSat payload. I'm now leading a team delivering two 2U CubeSats for the Australian Universities Rocketry Competition and the International Rocketry Engineering Competition.


Outside coursework, I was a Track Winner at the Cambridge EduX Hackathon and placed top 50 globally at Microsoft's AI for Good Hackathon. Right now I'm designing a prosthetic thumb for my grandfather, the most personal engineering project I've taken on. I also mentor high school Maths and Physics students and train new tutors.


I'm looking for robotics/mechatronics internship opportunities to build on this experience, and would love to connect!
